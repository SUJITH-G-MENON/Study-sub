1. Study Sub – AI-Powered Study Assistant

Study Sub is an AI-powered educational application designed to make learning easier, smarter and more efficient. It aims to help students understand complex topics, simplify study materials and improve their learning experience through AI-assisted tools.

2.Project Objective

The main objective of Study Sub is to make studying more accessible and interactive by combining artificial intelligence with educational support.

3. Key Features

- AI Study Assistance: Get support with academic questions and topics.
- Simplified Learning: Make complex concepts easier to understand.
- Interactive Question Answering: Ask questions and receive helpful explanations.
- Smart Study Support: Designed to improve productivity and learning efficiency.
